How PSI is shown at outdoor recreation areas
Each recreation area gets a marker coloured by its region's predicted PSI, with the number inside. Courts and playgrounds from OpenStreetMap use the same colours as small dots. The activity guide is a simplified version of NEA's PSI health advisory bands, so check nea.gov.sg for official advice.
Click anywhere on the map, or tap a recreation area, court or playground, to see the predicted PSI for that spot.

Five linear regression models, one per NEA region, trained on Admiralty weather station data and each region's PSI, April 2014 to November 2017 (n = 979 days per region). Test MAE versus a naive baseline (tomorrow = today): North 5.71 vs 6.07, South 6.16 vs 6.68, East 5.84 vs 6.33, West 6.12 vs 6.62, Central 6.88 vs 7.44.

Limitations: PSI is reported per region, not per street, so any spot you pick gets its region's prediction. Region borders are approximated by whichever NEA region centre is nearest. The clean map is a simplified outline, not a survey-grade map. All regions use the same weather inputs. The live version uses the current instant weather reading in place of the daily summaries the models were trained on. The listed recreation areas are examples with approximate positions. Court and playground locations come from OpenStreetMap volunteers and may be incomplete.
